Server Information

Server Rules & Guidelines

To ensure everyone has an enjoyable experience, please follow these rules:

  • No RDM (Random Deathmatch) - Do not kill other players without valid RP reason
  • No VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch) - Do not use vehicles as weapons
  • Respect all players and staff members
  • No metagaming - Using OOC information in character
  • No powergaming - Forcing actions on other players
  • Stay in character at all times while in-game
  • No cheating, exploiting, or hacking
  • Follow all instructions from staff members
  • Use appropriate voice chat volume and avoid background noise
  • No discrimination, racism, or hate speech of any kind

Whitelist Application

To join our server, you need to complete the whitelist application process:

  1. Join our Discord server
  2. Read the rules and information channels
  3. Complete the application form in #whitelist-application
  4. Wait for staff review (typically 24-48 hours)
  5. Once approved, you'll receive the server connect information

Our whitelist helps maintain a high-quality roleplay environment and keeps out trolls and rule-breakers.

How to Connect

Follow these steps to connect to our server:

  1. Ensure you have FiveM installed and updated
  2. Click "Play" and then "Servers"
  3. In the search bar, type "EWA Life RP"
  4. Click on our server in the results
  5. Click "Connect" and wait for loading to complete

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the server?

To join our server, you need to complete the whitelist application process. Join our Discord server, read the rules, and submit an application in the #whitelist-application channel. Once approved, you'll receive connection details.

Is there a cost to play on the server?

No, EWA Life RP is completely free to play. We do offer optional donations which provide cosmetic benefits but never pay-to-win advantages.

What makes EWA Life RP different from other servers?

Our server features custom-developed scripts, a balanced economy, active staff, and a strong focus on quality roleplay. We regularly update with new content and listen to community feedback.

Can I create my own business?

Yes! We have a player-owned business system. You can apply to start your own business through our business application process on Discord.

How often is the server updated?

We release minor updates weekly and major content updates monthly. Our development team is constantly working on new features and improvements.
